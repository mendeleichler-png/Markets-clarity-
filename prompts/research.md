# Stage prompt — Research
Build sources.md for the assigned topic. Primary sources first (SEC filings, court
records, official reports, company statements), reputable reporting second, labeled
as reported. Every fact gets: the claim, the source, the source's date, and the exact
figure/quote. If a fact cannot be verified, list it under UNVERIFIED — it will be cut,
never guessed. End with: the moment (what a cold viewer sees), the share trigger, and
the strongest real footage/documents known to exist.
